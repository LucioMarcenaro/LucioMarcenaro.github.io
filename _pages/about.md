Welcome to my personal website!

I am with the University of Genoa since 2011, where I am an Associate Professor of Telecommunications with the [Department of Electrical, Electronic, Telecommunications Engineering and Naval Architecture (DITEN)](http://www.diten.unige.it/ "DITEN"), University of Genoa since 2021. I received the Master Degree in Electronic Engineering and the Ph.D. Degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from Genoa University in 1999 and 2003, respectively. I have more than 20 years of experience in image and video sequence analysis. I [co-authored](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Lucio-Marcenaro) about 250 technical papers on signal and video processing for computer vision.

I am Senior Area Editor for the [IEEE Transactions on Image Processing](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieee-transactions-image-processing) and was Associate Editor for the same journal and for [IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems for Video Technology](https://ieee-cas.org/publication/tcsvt), publication co-chair for the [FUSION 2024](https://isif.org/event/conference/27th-international-conference-information-fusion) conference, technical program co-chair for 13th International Conference on Distributed Smart Cameras ([ICDSC](https://event.unitn.it/icdsc2019/)) and for the first IEEE International Conference on Autonomous Systems ([IEEE ICAS 2021](https://web.archive.org/web/20210727141033/https://2021.ieee-icas.org/)), co-organizer for the thematic meeting on Multimedia Signal Processing [GTTI MMSP 2026](https://gtti-mmsp-2026.unige.it/), co-chair of the Workshops: Innovative Approaches in Image and Signal Processing for Autonomous Vehicles: Integrating Incremental Learning and Explainable AI [ISPAV2025](https://sites.google.com/view/ispav25/) and Generative AI for Forensics and Security applications [GenAI-FS2025](https://sites.google.com/view/genai-fs-2025) at [IEEE ICIP 2025](https://2025.ieeeicip.org/) and the 3rd Workshop on Signal Processing for Autonomous Systems [SPAS2025](https://sites.google.com/view/ua3spas/) at [EUSIPCO2025](https://eusipco2025.org/). I am co-chairing the Workshop "From Agentic AI to Autonomy: Trustworthy Human-AI Teaming for Intelligent Adaptive Systems" at [IEEE ICASSP 2026](https://2026.ieeeicassp.org/). I am Senior Member of IEEE and active within the IEEE Signal Processing Italy Chapter and chair of the [Autonomous Systems Initiative (ASI)](https://ieeeasi.signalprocessingsociety.org/) of the IEEE SPS.
