I am an Associate Professor of Telecommunications at the [Department of Electrical, Electronic, Telecommunications Engineering and Naval Architecture (DITEN)](https://diten.unige.it/ "DITEN") of the University of Genoa, Italy. My work focuses on signal and video processing for computer vision, cognitive radio and autonomous systems.

I received the Master's degree in Electronic Engineering and the Ph.D. degree in Electronic and Computer Engineering from the University of Genoa in 1999 and 2003, respectively. From 2003 to 2010 I was CEO and Development Manager of TechnoAware srl. I joined the University of Genoa as Assistant Professor in 2011 and have been Associate Professor since 2021. In 2023 I obtained the National Scientific Qualification (ASN) as Full Professor in Telecommunications (09/IINF-03).

I have over 25 years of experience in signal processing and image sequence analysis and have co-authored [more than 220 papers](/publications/) in international journals, conferences and books. I am a Senior Member of the IEEE.

## Research interests

- Video processing for event recognition, and detection and localization of objects in complex scenes
- Self-awareness, anomaly detection and decision making for autonomous systems and vehicles
- Distributed heterogeneous sensors, multisensor data fusion and ambient intelligence
- Cognitive radio, integrated sensing and communication, and physical-layer security
- Bio-inspired cognitive systems and generative models
